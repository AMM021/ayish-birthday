Tsuki House

Ayish's dream house and garden. Tsuki means moon. The house has no box in it: a long crescent of rooms curves around a koi pond, a round tower holds the stair and an indoor tree, a rounded east wing holds the kitchen, and an oval bedroom floats above the crescent. Curved earth walls face outward and full-height glass faces the pond. Everything is drawn to scale in metres.

Concept

Sheet 01

A crescent around water

The sunroom, library and living room follow one curve around the koi pond. On the outside, thick rammed-earth walls give shelter, with small deep windows onto the gravel garden. On the inside, a continuous glass wall and a curved engawa open every room to the pond.

Forms, not boxes

A round tower with a spiral stair around a maple. A stadium-shaped east wing with a curved glass dining end. An oval bedroom pod, clad in timber, cantilevered over the crescent. Its roof sweeps like a leaf. Planted roofs and a curving roof terrace step down toward the garden.

Nature inside

Every main room looks onto a part of the garden made for it. The library looks onto the gravel garden and the pond, the living room onto the pond, the round dining room onto the pergola, the sunroom onto the jacarandas, and the bedroom onto the sunset and the city.

Materials, light and doors

Sheet 05

Lighting (no more glare)

  • Light is baked in Blender (Cycles) for a soft sunset, real bounce light and real shadows in every room.
  • Indoor fixtures use warm 2700 K hidden cove light, wall washers and pendants with glowing shades. Only the small bulbs bloom, and their brightness is set per room, so nothing blinds her.
  • Only the orb and doors are lit live. The orb's glow is softened when it is close to the camera.

Doors that work

  • Front door: a 1.2 m oak pivot door. Glass sliding doors to the engawa, the pergola, the terrace and the balcony.
  • Inside: shoji sliders to the library and tea room, and oak doors to the bath, WC, studio, bedroom and wardrobe.
  • How they open: each one opens smoothly as she walks up and closes behind her. While a door is open she can walk through it; while it is closed it is solid.
  • The round anniversary moon door stays sealed, with its note.

    Levels 3 and 4, and the fragment moment

    Sheet 06 · gameplay

    Level 3 · Stars: “The Sky Remembers”

    • 1. Melody: a night lake with lily-pad stepping stones and seven floating star-lanterns. The orb hums a melody, and each star is one note. She walks to the stars in the order she heard them. There are three rounds of 3, 4 and 5 notes. It's a memory game played by ear, not by sight, so it's different from the gates level.
    • 2. Bridge: each melody she finishes lights part of a bridge of light across the water to the heart island. If she slips off a stone, she gently reappears on the last stone she was on.
    • 3. The heart: on the island she walks the nine heart stars in order, with the outline drawn on the ground. Then a shower of shooting stars, and the camera rises.

    Level 4 · Petals: “The Storm”

    • Act 1: she stays in the light along a blossom path, as it is now, but gentler.
    • Act 2, the wind: gusts really push her sideways and petals hide the path. The light shrinks and weaves between stone lanterns and fallen branches she has to walk around.
    • Act 3, the storm: the light splits in two, and only the true one hums. She follows it across a stream bridge to a giant sakura tree, which bursts into bloom.
    • Checkpoints: each act has its own checkpoint, so failing only restarts that act. It's hard without being cruel.

    Fragment animation (every level)

    The level-complete chime plays and the world holds still for a moment.
    The fragment's code block (for H: heart.connect(ayish)…) types itself in glowing script in the air in front of her.
    The lines fold into one bright letter, H.
    The letter flies in an arc to the H·O·M·E bar, leaving a trail of sparkles.
    The tile lights up with a pop, then the orb says its fragment line.

    The new levels need a few new orb lines. I'll draft them for your approval before writing them, as before.

    How it gets built

    Sheet 07

    Pipeline

    • These drawings come from one data file. The same file drives Blender, so the game is built to exactly this plan.
    • Blender builds the walls, the curved walls, the stairs, the windows and the joinery. Its scripts model the beds, kitchen, bath and toilet fixtures, since no free set has good ones.
    • The furniture, plants, trees and rocks are free CC0 models from Poly Haven. The floors, plaster, stone, fabric and tiles are CC0 textures from ambientCG.
    • Blender lays everything out and checks each piece against walls and other pieces, so nothing pokes through. Then it bakes the lighting and exports compressed files for the browser.
